How the cleaning works. Prices are matched against a strict money grammar (so $1,2x9.00 is rejected rather than silently read as 129.00), then normalised from US (1,299.00) and European (1.299,00) layouts and converted to USD with the rates in config.json. Dates are tried against the configured formats in order; impossible dates are rejected. Duplicate SKUs are collapsed to the most complete record. Statistical outliers are flagged for review, never deleted.
