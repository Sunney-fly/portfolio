# Returns & Refunds

## The 30-day return window
You may return most items within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full
refund. The return window starts on the day the carrier marks the parcel as
delivered. Items must be unused, in their original packaging and with all
accessories included.

## How to start a return
1. Open "Orders" in your Brightpath account and select the order.
2. Click "Return or replace items" and choose a reason for the return.
3. Print the prepaid return label that we generate for you.
4. Hand the parcel to any carrier drop-off point within 14 days of creating
   the label.

Return shipping is free for defective, damaged or incorrect items. For
change-of-mind returns we deduct EUR 4.95 from the refund to cover the label.

## Refund timing
We inspect returns within 2 business days of arrival at our warehouse. Once
approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method. Card refunds
appear within 3-5 business days, PayPal refunds within 1-2 business days, and
bank transfers can take up to 10 business days depending on your bank.

## Non-returnable items
For hygiene and safety reasons the following cannot be returned: opened
mattresses and pillows, personal care devices, gift cards, and perishable
goods. Clearance items marked "final sale" are also excluded. Perishable and
final-sale items are never eligible for a refund, even if they arrive damaged
- in that case contact support within 48 hours and we will send a replacement.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the unwanted item for a refund and
place a new order for the item you want. This is the fastest way to get the
product you need, because it does not wait for the return to be inspected.

## Damaged or incorrect items
If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, contact
support within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo. We
ship a replacement immediately and cover all shipping costs.
