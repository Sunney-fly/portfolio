# Account & Order Changes

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can cancel or change an order free of charge while its status is still
"Processing". Once the status becomes "Shipped" the order can no longer be
changed and you should use the returns process instead. To change a delivery
address after dispatch, contact support and we will ask the carrier for a
redirect, which costs EUR 6.50 and may delay delivery by 1-2 days.

## Resetting your password
Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. The reset email arrives
within 5 minutes; check your spam folder if it does not. For security reasons
a password reset link expires after 60 minutes and can only be used once.

## Updating account details
Email address, delivery addresses and marketing preferences can be changed
under "Account" > "Settings". Changing the email address requires confirming a
code sent to the old address. To delete your account and all associated data,
use "Account" > "Privacy" > "Delete account"; deletion is completed within 30
days and cancels any active subscriptions.

## Order history and invoices
Receipts and VAT invoices for the last 7 years are available under "Orders".
Guest orders (placed without an account) can be looked up with the order
number and the email address used at checkout.

## Loyalty programme
Brightpath Plus members earn 2 points per EUR 1 spent; 100 points equal
EUR 5 of store credit. Points are credited 14 days after delivery, once the
return window for an item has closed. Points expire 24 months after they are
credited.
