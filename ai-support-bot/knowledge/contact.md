# Contacting Support

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 CET, and
Saturday 10:00 to 16:00 CET. We are closed on Sundays and on Dutch public
holidays. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.

## Channels and response times
Live chat on the website is the fastest option, with a typical first response
under 3 minutes during opening hours. Email to support@brightpath-goods.example
is answered within 8 business hours. Phone support is available on
+31 20 555 0148 for order issues only.

## What to include
To resolve an issue in one contact, include your order number (format
BP-XXXXXXX), the email address used at checkout, and a clear description of
the problem. For damaged goods, attach a photo. For payment issues, include
the last four digits of the card and the approximate charge date.

## Escalation and complaints
If you are not satisfied with the first response, ask for the ticket to be
escalated to a support supervisor. Escalated complaints are reviewed within 3
business days. If the issue is still unresolved after 30 days you may refer it
to the European Consumer Centre or to the Dutch consumer dispute committee.

## Business and wholesale enquiries
Retailers and corporate buyers should use the wholesale form on our website
rather than the consumer support queue. Wholesale enquiries are handled by a
separate team with a response time of 2 business days.
