# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery options and times
Brightpath Goods ships from our warehouse in Rotterdam, Netherlands.
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the EU and costs EUR 4.95.
Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs EUR 12.95.
Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day are dispatched the same day.
We do not dispatch orders on Saturdays, Sundays or public holidays.

## Free shipping threshold
Standard shipping is free on every order over EUR 60.00 after discounts.
The free shipping threshold does not apply to bulky items such as furniture,
which always carry a EUR 19.95 freight surcharge.

## Order tracking
As soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse you receive an email with a
tracking link. Tracking information can take up to 12 hours to become active
in the carrier's system. You can also see the current status at any time under
"Orders" in your Brightpath account.

## International shipping
We currently deliver to 31 European countries. Deliveries outside the EU
(Iceland, Norway, Switzerland and the United Kingdom) take 5-9 business days
and may be subject to import duties and customs fees, which are paid by the
customer. We do not ship to PO boxes outside the EU.

## Missing or delayed parcels
If tracking has not updated for more than 7 business days, contact support and
we will open a trace with the carrier. A parcel is officially considered lost
after 21 days, and we will then reship the order at no cost or issue a full
refund, whichever you prefer.
