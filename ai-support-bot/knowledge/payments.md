# Payments, Pricing & Promotions

## Accepted payment methods
Brightpath Goods accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro, PayPal,
Apple Pay, Google Pay, iDEAL, Bancontact and SEPA bank transfer. All card
payments are processed by our PCI-DSS Level 1 payment provider; we never store
full card numbers on our servers.

## Paying in instalments
Orders between EUR 100 and EUR 3,000 can be split into 3 interest-free
instalments. The first instalment is charged at checkout and the remaining two
are charged after 30 and 60 days. Instalment plans are available in Germany,
the Netherlands, Belgium, Austria and Spain.

## Failed or declined payments
A payment can be declined because of an expired card, an incorrect billing
address, insufficient funds or a bank security block. We retry a failed
payment automatically once after 24 hours. If the second attempt fails the
order is cancelled and any reserved stock is released. No money is taken for
an order that is cancelled this way.

## Promo codes and gift cards
Only one promo code can be applied per order and promo codes cannot be
combined with clearance pricing. Gift cards can be combined with promo codes.
If a promo code is not working, check that it has not expired and that your
cart meets the minimum order value stated in the offer.

## Prices, VAT and invoices
All prices shown include VAT for EU customers. The VAT rate is determined by
the delivery country. A VAT invoice is generated automatically and is
available as a PDF under "Orders" > "Invoice". Business customers can add a
company name and VAT number in the account settings before ordering.
