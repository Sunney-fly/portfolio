# Warranty & Product Care

## What the warranty covers
Every Brightpath-branded product carries a 2-year limited warranty starting on
the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures
under normal use, including electrical faults, broken hinges and seams, and
premature battery failure.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear and
tear, cosmetic scratches, damage caused by unauthorised repair, or products
used outside their stated purpose. Consumable parts such as filters, blades
and bulbs are excluded, but we sell replacements at cost to warranty holders.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number and a description of the fault. We may
ask for a short video or photos. If the fault is confirmed, we repair the item
free of charge, or replace it if repair is not possible. Warranty shipping in
both directions is paid by Brightpath Goods.

## Extended warranty
You can extend the warranty to 4 years within 60 days of purchase for 12% of
the product price. The extended warranty is transferable if you gift the
product, and it includes one free accidental-damage repair.

## Repairs outside warranty
Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted before any work begins. A standard
diagnostic fee of EUR 29 applies and is credited against the repair if you
accept the quote. Typical turnaround for a repair is 10-15 business days.
