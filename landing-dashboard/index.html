    Questions

    Answered before you ask

    How long does it take to get my data in?

    Most teams are live the same day. Connect Shopify, Amazon or WooCommerce with one click, or upload a CSV of your catalogue and stock counts. Stocklight maps your columns automatically and asks you to confirm anything ambiguous before it writes a single record.

    Do I need a data team or an analyst?

    No. Every forecast ships with a plain-English explanation of what moved it — seasonality, a promotion, a new channel or a price change. If a number looks wrong you can open the inputs behind it in two clicks.

    How accurate is the forecasting, honestly?

    Across our customer base the median 30-day SKU forecast lands within 12% of actuals, and we show you a confidence band on every chart rather than a single line. New SKUs with no history start from category analogues and improve as orders arrive.

    What happens to my data if I cancel?

    You can export your full catalogue, order history and forecasts as CSV at any time, including on the day you cancel. We keep your data for 30 days after cancellation so an accidental downgrade is reversible, then delete it.

    Can I try it on a small catalogue first?

    Yes — the 14-day trial includes the full Growth feature set and unlimited SKUs, so you can test it on your messiest category. No credit card, and you can downgrade to Starter without losing history.
